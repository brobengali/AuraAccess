How to Use AuraAccess

A complete reference guide for individuals with quadriplegia, ALS, motor neuron conditions, and caregivers.

1. Quick Start in 3 Simple Steps

No complicated setup or internet connection required. Everything runs locally on your PC.

  1. Position your webcam: Place your camera roughly at eye level, 50–70 cm in front of you with decent frontal lighting.
  2. Confirm nose tracking: Look at the Live Sensor Cockpit above. You should see a green dot tracking your nose tip in real time.
  3. Tap Shift to take control: Press the Shift key once on your keyboard (or click "Grant Access"). A high chime will sound and the cursor will immediately follow your head movement.
⌨️

2. Shift Tap Safety Clutch (No Holding Required)

AuraAccess never traps or hijacks your cursor. You retain 100% control with a single key tap.

Single Tap Shift
Access Granted (Active): Cursor steering and gestures engage immediately. An ascending high chime (1175 Hz) confirms activation.
Tap Shift Again
Access Declined (Safety Standby): Cursor freezes instantly in place. Gestures are locked. A descending low chime (587 Hz) confirms safety lock. Physical mouse and keyboard are completely free.
Tip for Caregivers: If you need to use the computer, simply tap Shift to lock AuraAccess, or click "Decline Access" on the dashboard.
🎯

3. Swift Head-Mouse Cursor Navigation

The cursor moves fluidly like a high-DPI optical mouse with zero lag and built-in pointer acceleration.

  • Subtle Head Tilts: Gentle nods (up/down) and turns (left/right) steer the mouse across your desktop.
  • Non-Linear Acceleration: Small head motions give single-pixel precision for clicking small text or checkboxes; quick head turns swiftly fling the cursor across dual monitors without straining your neck.
  • Adaptive Tremor Filter: High-frequency muscle twitches, Parkinsonian tremors, and accidental spasms are automatically dampened by the 2D Kalman filter.
  • Sensitivity Slider: Adjust responsiveness from 0.8x (conservative) to 5.0x (ultra-responsive) depending on your comfort range.
⏱️

4. Dwell Click Accumulator (Hover to Click)

Click any button, link, or desktop icon simply by pausing your cursor over it.

1. Steer onto target: Move the mouse cursor over the folder, button, or link you want to click.
2. Rest for 0.9 seconds: Keep your head reasonably still inside the 48-pixel target zone.
3. Visual 360° Ring: Watch the yellow/green progress ring complete a circle around your nose tip in the camera feed.
4. Instant Click & Chime: A crisp click sound plays and a native Windows Left Click is dispatched instantly.
5. 0.8s Safety Cooldown: Dwell pauses for 0.8s after clicking to prevent accidental repeated clicks while reading.
👁️

6. Emergency Caregiver SOS Alarm

Designed for locked-in or bedridden individuals who need urgent attention from a family member or nurse.

Sustained Eye Closure (3.5s)
Close both eyes continuously for 3.5 seconds to trigger the emergency siren.
Rapid Distress Blinks
Blink rapidly 5 times in 2.5 seconds to instantly trigger the alarm.
Local Audio Siren
A loud 900 Hz emergency tone sounds through your PC speakers to alert caregivers across the house, even when headphones are unplugged.
📐

7. Personalized Mobility Calibration

No hardcoded thresholds. The system adapts to each individual's unique physical range of motion.

  • Click Recalibrate Mobility Range on the dashboard or run python calibration/run_calibration.py.
  • The visual calibration wizard guides you through 5 comfortable poses: Resting Neutral, Look Left, Look Right, Look Up, and Look Down.
  • Even if a user can only move their head by 3 degrees, the normalizer stretches that 3-degree range across the full 100% width of the desktop!
  • Multiple profiles can be created for different users (e.g. "Grandpa", "Patient 1", "Guest") using the + New User button in the top bar.
🖥️

8. Controlling Other Desktop Applications

Use AuraAccess to browse the web, write documents, open games, and control any Windows application.

  • Always-on-Top Floating Dock: Double click START_AURA_ACCESS.bat or run python ui/assistive_dock.py to launch the floating accessibility ribbon.
  • On-Screen Keyboard: Dwell-click the "OSK Keyboard" button on the floating ribbon to type emails and search queries without a physical keyboard.
  • Application Switcher: Right-wink or dwell on "Switch App" to cycle between your open windows.
  • Web Browsing: Open Chrome, Edge, or Firefox. Dwell on links to open them, open your mouth to scroll down news feeds or PDFs.
🔧

9. Troubleshooting & FAQs

Quick solutions to common operational questions.

Q: Cursor is not moving when I move my head?

Make sure you have tapped Shift to grant access. The header badge must say 🟢 ACTIVE. Also verify that the green dot in the camera stream is tracking your nose.

Q: Cursor is moving too slowly or too fast?

Adjust the Head Motion Responsiveness (Sensitivity) slider on the dashboard. 2.4x is recommended for standard displays, 3.5x–4.0x for multi-monitor setups.

Q: Can someone else use the mouse while AuraAccess is running?

Yes! Simply tap Shift once to lock AuraAccess. In safety standby, the physical mouse and keyboard work with 100% normal native Windows behavior.

Q: Does any camera video leave my computer?

Zero. AuraAccess is completely air-gapped and local-first. Video frames are processed in system RAM and immediately discarded. No data is ever saved to disk or transmitted to any server.
